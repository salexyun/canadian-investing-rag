## Asking a question

Open the Streamlit app and type a question, e.g.:

- "I became a permanent resident 8 months ago — can I open an FHSA?"
- "What's the difference between TFSA and RRSP contribution room?"
- "Do RESP grants require the child to be a Canadian citizen?"

Each answer should include:

- A direct response
- Citations back to the source page(s) it was grounded in
- A feedback control (👍 / 👎)
